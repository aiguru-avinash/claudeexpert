Q75D4Intermediate

Objective: Define evaluation metrics (accuracy, latency, cost, safety, security)

Scenario

A sponsor says the assistant must be "99% accurate."

Question

What is the best architect response?

  1. AAgree and promise 99% overall.
  2. BClarify what "accurate" means per task and segment, how it will be measured and on which golden set, and what an error costs; then record thresholds as acceptance criteria.
  3. CRefuse to give any target.
  4. DUse the vendor's benchmark score as the target.
Show answer & explanation

Correct answer B

Explanation

Vague targets need operational definitions and failure-cost context before they become acceptance criteria.

Why not the others

A over-commits blindly; C is unhelpful; D isn't specific to your use case.

Architectural principle

Specific, measurable, relevant criteria.

Reference

https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/define-success

Q76D4Advanced

Objective: Define evaluation metrics (accuracy, latency, cost, safety, security)

Scenario

A candidate release scores 92% on task accuracy (threshold 90%) but yields three critical policy violations in the 500-case adversarial set (threshold: zero).

Question

What is the right decision?

  1. AShip; accuracy passes.
  2. BShip with a note to monitor.
  3. CAverage the metrics and ship if the mean exceeds 90.
  4. DBlock the release: safety is a hard gate; fix and re-run.
Show answer & explanation

Correct answer D

Explanation

Safety criteria are pass/fail gates, not tradeable against accuracy.

Why not the others

A–C treat a gate as optional.

Architectural principle

Multidimensional criteria with non-negotiable safety gates.
